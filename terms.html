Booking, cancellation and privacy

Candidate information for review, 27 September 2026. Paid booking is not open. The provider setup, final business disclosures and privacy details must be verified before this becomes the live booking policy.

Your appointment

Jonathan Bonello, trading as Tachyharmonic, offers one-to-one online sessions for thinking and exploring the use of AI together. One hour costs £30 supported, £50 standard or £70 solidarity. Two hours costs £100 standard or £140 solidarity. The whole price is shown before you pay. A two-hour session is one appointment.

A time shown as available is provisional until reserved and confirmed. Payment is handled by Stripe. Payment alone does not confirm an appointment: wait for the booking confirmation. If payment succeeds but an appointment cannot be secured, we will show a failed or pending status, arrange the appropriate full refund and contact you. An uncertain provider response remains pending while it is checked.

Keep your private booking-management link. It allows you to check status and request cancellation. Do not share it. You can also contact jonathan@tachyharmonic.ai about any booking or payment problem.

Cancellation and rescheduling

At least 24 hours before your appointment, the controlled cancellation route provides a full refund under Jonathan’s policy, subject to your statutory rights. Both hours of a two-hour appointment are cancelled together. If cancellation or refund processing is delayed, the status will remain pending until the provider confirms the action.

Under 24 hours, your request goes to Jonathan for personal review. Submitting a request does not itself cancel the appointment or confirm a refund. There is no automatic blanket forfeiture. Your statutory rights take precedence over this appointment policy.

For rescheduling, email Jonathan. A new time must be checked before a move is confirmed.

Your statutory cancellation rights

For an eligible online service contract, you normally have 14 days after the day the contract is made to cancel without giving a reason. This period runs from the contract date, not backwards from the appointment date. You may email Jonathan with a clear statement that you wish to cancel; using the online route is optional. If you use the online route under 24 hours ahead, Jonathan will review your statutory rights as well as the appointment policy.

If you want preparation or the session to begin during that period, we ask separately for your express request. If you then cancel after work has begun, a proportionate amount for services actually supplied may be payable where the law permits. This is assessed by Jonathan and is not an automatic fee. Your statutory change-of-mind cancellation right ends after the service is fully performed only where you requested early performance and acknowledged that consequence.

The separate, initially unticked early-performance choice is: “I expressly request preparation and my session to begin within my 14-day cancellation period. I understand that, if I cancel after work begins, a proportionate amount for work actually supplied may be payable where the law permits, and that my statutory cancellation right ends once the service is fully performed.”

Where a statutory refund is due, it will be made without undue delay and within the applicable legal deadline, normally using the original payment method. “Refund initiated” means the payment provider has accepted the refund; it does not mean the money has already appeared at your bank.

You can use this cancellation statement, although it is not required: “To Jonathan Bonello, Tachyharmonic, jonathan@tachyharmonic.ai: I give notice that I cancel my service contract. Booking reference: ____. Contract date: ____. Name: ____. Address: ____. Date: ____.”

Booking privacy

Jonathan is responsible for the booking information. Contact jonathan@tachyharmonic.ai with privacy questions or requests.

The candidate collects your name and email to arrange and deliver the appointment, and records the selected time, rate, payment references, cancellation state and your early-performance choice. The basis for information necessary to provide the service is taking steps at your request and performing the contract. Necessary accounting records support legal obligations; limited security and operational records protect and run the service.

Your note is optional and limited to 1,200 characters. Jonathan can read it through a protected operator route to prepare for the session. Please avoid health information, passwords and other sensitive details. No AI note processing, recording or transcription is included in this booking flow. The note is kept in the private booking store and is excluded from payment and scheduling metadata, calendar entries, routine emails and logs.

The intended providers are Cloudflare for the private booking service, Stripe for payment, Google Calendar for scheduling and Google Meet for video calls, and Resend for transactional messages. Each receives only the information needed for its role. Card details go to Stripe; this website does not store them. The separate Porkbun mailbox handles human correspondence.

In the candidate, abandoned or failed booking notes are cleared by the scheduled cleanup, and confirmed-session notes are cleared 30 days after the appointment. Provider copies and backups have separate retention. Final retention periods for contact, financial and audit records, international-transfer safeguards and provider processing terms remain launch review items; this draft does not promise immediate deletion from backups.

You may ask for access, correction or deletion, restriction, portability or object where applicable. Some records may need to be retained for legal obligations or disputes. You may complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office. The booking service makes no AI decisions about you.

The browser keeps only a private recovery token and opaque booking references in session storage to recover an interrupted checkout. Treat the management link as private. Name, email and note are not deliberately saved in browser storage by the booking code.

Review before opening booking

The final policy must include the trader’s verified geographic contact address, the actual provider and transfer details, record-retention periods, and the confirmed contract-formation point. These require Jonathan’s review against the deployed flow before the booking switch is enabled.
