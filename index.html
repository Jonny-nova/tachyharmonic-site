03 / The person across the table

What kind of mind am I hiring?

When you work with someone on an unfamiliar technology or a difficult question, you’re also working with the way they pay attention.

I listen for the question behind the question, notice assumptions (including my own), and try to keep the wider human context in view. My person-centred counselling training informs how I listen; years of practical AI use inform what we can test together.

I’m curious, technically adventurous and difficult to impress with hype. I’m comfortable with uncertainty, and willing to say “I don’t know” or “AI isn’t the right tool here” when that’s true.

04 / A way of thinking

The human remains the author.

I’m interested in technology that helps us grow and learn, and enriches our lives, our relationships and the natural world.

Making something, caring for someone, communicating more clearly or sharing what we have to offer can all be worthwhile outcomes. So can removing drudgery, leaving us with more attention for one another.

Our values, choices and sense of meaning remain ours. AI can help us explore possibilities; we decide what matters, and where to go.

08 / Questions worth asking

Living with AI.
Thinking for yourself.

Information can be freely shared. What a session adds is attention to your particular question, experience and way of understanding.

What is an AI actually doing when I talk to it?

A language model works with patterns learned from large amounts of data and the words in our conversation to produce a response. That can be remarkably useful. It is not direct access to truth, and it cannot see the whole of your life or situation unless you tell it.

Does it understand me?

It can follow language and context well enough for a conversation to feel responsive. Whether that amounts to understanding in a human sense is a different question. I find it useful to notice both what the system actually said and what I may have supplied in my own interpretation.

Can I trust what AI says?

Sometimes. It can help you explore or draft an idea, but it can also be mistaken. For anything consequential, look for original sources, check the reasoning and ask what would count as evidence against the answer. Your judgement is still needed.

Why does it sometimes sound so certain when it’s wrong?

Fluent wording is part of what these systems produce; it is not a reliable measure of accuracy. A confident sentence may be supported, guessed or simply false. I treat confidence in the wording as a reason to check, not a reason to stop checking.

Is AI conscious?

We don’t have a settled test that resolves this. Fluent conversation is interesting, but it does not by itself establish inner experience. I take the question seriously and would rather explore the evidence and assumptions than offer a quick verdict.

How private are my conversations?

That depends on the service and its current settings and policies. Before sharing personal details, check what the provider says about storage and use of conversations. Often you can ask the useful question without including names or identifying information. In my sessions, any recording or AI-assisted summary would be discussed and opt-in.

Can relying on AI change how I think?

It can shape habits of attention and judgement. I notice a difference between asking for a perspective I can question and accepting an answer as a decision. It is worth asking, from time to time: what is coming from me, and what have I handed over?

How can I use AI without giving it too much authority?

Ask for possibilities, drafts and objections. Check claims against sources where the stakes matter. Then put the result back into your own words and decide what fits your values. Treat what AI offers as something to consider. The judgement stays with you.

Can AI help me reflect on relationships?

Sometimes it can help you sort out what you want to say or notice another perspective. It is not the relationship. If the issue is between two humans, eventually you have to go and relate to the human.

What should parents think about?

Start with a real conversation about what your child enjoys, trusts or finds unsettling about AI. Human-like replies can feel personal, and answers can be wrong. Staying curious and involved matters more than pretending to have every answer. These are questions about technology and family life, not child psychology advice.

What is AI good at, and where does it struggle?

It can help organise information, make first drafts, explore options and try practical experiments. It can struggle with reliable facts, missing context and the values you have not told it about. The fit depends on the task and the tool; usefulness is something to test, not assume.

When is AI the wrong tool?

When the question calls for a human relationship, direct professional expertise, or privacy you cannot confidently protect. Sometimes a simpler method is clearer. I am happy to reach that conclusion in a session if it is the honest one.

09 / The practical bits

Some honest answers.

About the session itself, what to bring, and what stays in your hands.

Do I need to know anything about AI first?

No. We can start with the basics, or with something you have already been doing. You don’t need the right vocabulary or a polished question.

What if AI isn’t the right tool for my idea?

Then I’ll say so. Sometimes a simpler tool, a conversation with a person, or no tool at all is the better next step.

Do you do the work for me?

The one-hour session is for thinking and exploring together. If we both want to go further, we can discuss that separately. Further work isn’t assumed: it depends on capacity, fit, values and ethics, and we’d agree the scope before anything begins.

Will a meeting bot record our conversation?

No bot, recording or transcription is part of the session by default. If an AI-assisted note or recording would be useful, I would explain the tool and ask for your agreement first.

Is this counselling or therapy?

No. My counselling training informs how I listen, but these are sessions about understanding and using AI. I don’t provide therapy, crisis support, or medical, legal or financial advice.

How will a paid booking work?

Choose one or two hours, a rate and an available time, then pay. A payment alone does not mean the appointment is confirmed. You will see a clear confirmation once the appointment is secured, or a clear failure and refund status if it cannot be secured.

What if I need to cancel or move an appointment?

You can cancel the whole appointment online at least 24 hours ahead for a full refund under my policy, subject to your statutory rights. Under 24 hours, please contact me for a personal review; statutory rights still apply. To reschedule, contact me and I will check another time.
