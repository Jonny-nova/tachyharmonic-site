A human-centred thinking practice

AI is powerful, strange, and moving very fast.

You don’t have to figure it all out by yourself.

I help people understand AI, use it more confidently, and explore what it might genuinely be useful for in their lives, work and creative projects.

What this is

Time with a human mind, with AI available as part of the toolkit. A well-held hour to think together about something that matters to you.

  • Why can it sound so certain when it’s wrong?
  • How do I stay grounded while using it?

We’ll follow your questions, at your pace.

03 / The person across the table

Curious by temperament.
Hard to impress with hype.
Quite comfortable with “perhaps”.

What kind of mind am I hiring?

I’m Jonathan. When you work with someone on an unfamiliar technology or a difficult question, you’re also working with the way they pay attention.

I’ve spent several years working deeply with AI in everyday life and in human-centred community systems. My training in person-centred counselling informs how I listen; my contemplative practice has taught me to notice assumptions, including my own. Neither makes this therapy, and I won’t pretend to have a neat answer to every question.

I’m interested in what these tools make possible, and in what we need to keep hold of as we use them. I live in rural Wales, grow food, and tend to think technology should make more room for life rather than swallow it.

05 / Questions

Some honest answers.

Useful information shouldn’t be hidden behind a booking. Here are a few things people often wonder about.

Do I need to know anything about AI first?

No. We can start with the basics, or with something you have already been doing. You don’t need the right vocabulary or a polished question.

Can I trust what an AI tells me?

It can be useful, persuasive and mistaken at the same time. For important claims, check the original sources and keep your own judgement in the loop. We can talk about practical ways to do that.

What if AI isn’t the right tool for my idea?

Then I’ll say so. Sometimes a simpler tool, a conversation with a person, or no tool at all is the better next step.

Do you do the work for me?

We can explore and build things together during a session, but this isn’t an outsourced project service. The project and its decisions remain yours.

Will a meeting bot record our conversation?

No recording or transcription happens as a default part of the session. If an AI-assisted note or recording would be useful, I would explain the tool and ask for your agreement first.

Is this counselling or therapy?

No. My counselling training informs how I listen, but these are sessions about understanding and using AI. I don’t provide therapy, crisis support, or medical, legal or financial advice.
